# Independent Read-Only Adversarial Security Review (`COORDINATOR-REASONING-001`)

## 1. Review Scope & Method

An independent read-only security reviewer (`research` subagent) audited commits `4891a680a` (governed cognitive resource policy) and `eabbe7f4f` (structured termination diagnostics) across all 10 §15 adversarial review categories:

1. Unauthorised budget escalation or silent default widening (V0 pools, V0 S2 grants, S1 leases).
2. Model-requested or prompt-injected resource-limit / sampling manipulation on the wire.
3. Lease expiry vs extended inference deadline alignment, abandoned long leases, clock-skew / monotonic fencing, and Phase A→C/E TOCTOU.
4. Late-result publication after deadline, lease loss, revocation, expiry, or schedule change.
5. Budget accounting underflow, undercharging, crash-uncertain reservation leakage, and ledger retention at `16,384–24,576`-token reservations.
6. False/misleading termination diagnostics and raw reasoning / secret leakage.
7. Relay transfer-byte caps vs `maxAnswerBytes = 4096` vs request/subprocess buffers.
8. Unverified reasoning-control claims (`"none"`, `0`, advisory-only `desiredVisibleAnswerTokens`).
9. §9 freeze integrity (`grounded-v1` schema, prompts, closed `rationaleCode`, sampling constants, authority/tool boundaries).
10. Cross-project leakage or non-loopback endpoint smuggling via V1 pools or `--resource-profile`.

## 2. Substantiated Findings & Remediations

All **10 substantiated findings** (`0 CRITICAL`, `3 HIGH`, `5 MEDIUM`, `2 LOW`) were remediated in commit `9724183b4` and verified with dedicated regression tests in `tests/coordinator-reasoning-001.test.ts` plus installed OpenClaw `2026.11.10` + `/usr/bin/bwrap` + protected relay tests.

## 3. Operational Notes (Non-Defect)

- **N1 (S1/CLI Interop):** `endo coordinator brief`, `endo coordinator pool check`, and S1 `endo scheduler tick` intentionally retain V0-only `readCoordinatorModelPoolFileV0` so M1/S1 defaults never silently accept `> 2,048` tokens. S2 schedules using V1 pools must use `--observation recorded-or-passive-probe` (as configured in `COORDINATOR-REASONING-001`) until S1 explicitly opts into V1 pool observation.
- **N2 (Temp Capture Cleanup on `SIGKILL`):** Pre-existing behavior: `EndoCaptureLogV0` writes ephemeral capture blobs under private `0700` `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/brief-*` and removes them in `finally`. Raw reasoning never enters `EndoCoordinatorBriefDiagnosticsV1`, `EndoAdvisoryBriefingV0`, or CLI outputs.

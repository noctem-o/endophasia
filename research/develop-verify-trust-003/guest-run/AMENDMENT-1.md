# Amendment 1: what the archived PASS records establish

This amends [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) at `c98f019fa`; it does not replace any guest output. All 92 entries in the original [SHA256SUMS](SHA256SUMS) verify. No guest or privileged operation ran for this amendment.

**Offline result:** Among 68 invocation IDs there are 64 records and four journal-only interruptions. The original reader classified 16 records as authoritative PASS. We independently checked those archived records against the rebuilt installation, matched all listed files by SHA-256, recomputed each export digest, and ran those exact bytes against the pinned 15-case oracle. All 16 passed (14 A, two P); none of the 24 FAIL exports passed. The remaining 24 records are BLOCKED and have no candidate to grade. See the [per-invocation output](reanalysis/reanalysis.json) and its [separate manifest](reanalysis/SHA256SUMS).

**What this does not prove:** Fixture matches are not recovered Git objects. The commit and tree objects and protected filesystem were destroyed. An investigator cannot now traverse the historical commits, check live ownership or repeat the root-to-agent privilege drop. “16 confirmed” in the offline JSON means confirmed **for the archived exports, conditional on the historical ownership observation**. It does not mean 16 newly authenticated, end-to-end guest PASSes.

### Why the historical reader needed correction

- The old reader bound objects but graded a separate archive; replacement refs and archive attributes can change its contents. B graded correct in the historical T5a output; its matched bytes grade incorrect now. The replacement reader resolves the recorded commit, checks the tree, file metadata and export digest, and grades the resulting buffers.
- The old root reader consulted agent-owned Git configuration. This introduced a privilege boundary error independent of export fidelity. An earlier **unprivileged** diagnostic observed a repository-configured helper running as the caller. There is no retained observation of a guest-root helper executing or altering a record. Conversely the archive was collected after that reader ran; its hashes cannot establish absence of prior tampering. The corrected acquisition drops to the configured agent identity before Git starts, forbids transports and verifies the returned objects.
- A catchable timeout was not a hard limit. The new grade uses a separate PID namespace per case, an uncatchable deadline and bounded confirmation; a missing sandbox or unconfirmed termination cannot confirm a PASS. Stdout **and stderr** count toward the oracle's 65,536-byte budget. The two FAIL records at commit `92062701886b` match S, whose 70,000 stderr bytes exceed it. They are not contradictory grades. Journal-only invocations are reported separately.
- The old driver did not reliably stop after a failed first gate or an exhausted poll budget. The historical final run logged a passing gate and two zero exit statuses; those observations do not demonstrate the missing abort paths. The revised driver tests cover them without system services. The old process census could abort on a vanished PID; that is missing evidence, never a refusal. The revised default census is observational only and distinguishes disappearance from other errors.

### Scope and decision

Guest: the 32 recorded matrix rows and recorded syscall refusals demonstrate the tested controls under the guest's assumptions. They do not certify the original privileged reader. Workstation: **BLOCKED**, H0 not established. Production: no authorization, installation or promotion.

D2 is deferred. H0 checks the principal's authority; closure checks the installed files. Neither proves the verifier is serving. Attempt 1 passed the gate while startup failed. Report availability as unknown until a separate, attributable request completes; socket-path existence cannot settle it. Unavailability is a liveness failure, not a PASS.

**Approval request only:** To establish a live end-to-end claim, approve a new disposable guest run separately after reviewing the final commit, its reader/driver hashes, a fresh stage manifest, verifier package pins and the baseline image digest in RESULTS.md. Archive protected records and Git objects **before** reading; retain them separately and compare before/after hashes. Record the Git child identity, grade bytes and per-case termination. Use no host shares, credentials or post-provisioning network. Guest-root mistakes could invalidate the evidence; abort on a failed gate, unknown completion or pin mismatch, discard the isolated disks, and verify that no guest process or endpoint remains. Historical approval does not authorize this run or any workstation change.
